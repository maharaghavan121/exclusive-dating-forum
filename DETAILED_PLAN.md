# Exclusive Dating Forum – Comprehensive Planning Framework

---

## Sample Run of Show (3 Hours)

| Time | Segment | Details | Facilitation Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0:00 – 0:30** | Arrivals & Welcome | Casual drinks & mingling | Host welcome, set casual tone |
| **0:30 – 1:30** | Activity Part 1 | First round of teams/pairs | Rotate partners midway |
| **1:30 – 2:00** | Half-Time & Refreshments | Snacks, drinks, group regroup | Natural social reset |
| **2:00 – 2:45** | Activity Part 2 | Second activity round / tasting / finals | Mix up groups for new pairings |
| **2:45 – End** | Open Social & Hangout | Dessert, drinks, open mingling | No pressure, voluntary stay |

---

## Post-Event Follow-Up Options

1. **Option A (Double-Blind Mutual Match):** Attendees privately text the host the names of people they'd like to see again. Host connects mutual matches.
2. **Option B (Shared Directory / Group Chat):** Share an optional WhatsApp group / IG directory for attendees to connect naturally.
3. **Option C (Host 1-on-1 Check-Ins):** Host reaches out individually to collect feedback and facilitate warm introductions.
