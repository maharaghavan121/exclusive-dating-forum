# Exclusive Dating Forum – Planning Document

**Format:** 20 Friends (10 Women, 10 Men)  
**Status:** Brainstorming & Planning  

---

## 1. Core Concept

* **Goal:** Bring 20 single friends together for a fun, low-pressure event centered around interactive activities rather than awkward forced dating.
* **Cohort:** Balanced 10 women and 10 men from mutual circles.
* **Dynamic:** Structured activity rotations to naturally facilitate conversation, followed by open social time.

---

## 2. Activity Candidates

* **Pickleball Social:** High energy, fast round-robin partner rotations, natural icebreaker.
* **Make Pizza Together:** Collaborative, hands-on cooking in pairs/small groups, shared meal.
* **Cooking Competition:** Team challenge with mystery ingredients or tasting & judging.
