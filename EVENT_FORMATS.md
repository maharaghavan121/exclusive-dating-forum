# Event Formats & Rotation Mechanics

Detailed breakdown and group dynamics for the 3 core activity options.

---

## 1. Pickleball Social & Tournament

### Objective
High-energy, icebreaking activity where partners rotate continuously so everyone plays with and against everyone.

### Setup & Equipment
* **Courts:** 2–3 courts reserved for 2 hours.
* **Format:** Doubles round-robin.
* **Duration:** 10–12 minute mini-games (play to 7 or 11 points).

### Rotation Mechanics
* Each round pairs 1 Man + 1 Woman against another 1 Man + 1 Woman team.
* After each mini-game, switch partners across courts.
* Keep a non-playing "lounge / hydration bench" where 4 players rest, drink, and chat while waiting for the next rotation.

---

## 2. Pizza Making Night

### Objective
Collaborative, hands-on cooking in pairs/small groups where conversation happens naturally while prepping food.

### Setup & Stations
* **Prep Stations:** 4 stations (5 people per station: 2–3 men, 2–3 women).
* **Supplies:** Pre-made dough balls, diverse sauces, cheeses, and topping platters.
* **Ovens:** Standard home ovens with pizza stones/steels or outdoor pizza ovens (Ooni / Roccbox).

### Flow & Dynamics
1. **Station Pairing (30 mins):** Guests pair up to knead, stretch, and assemble custom signature pizzas.
2. **Baking & Sharing (30 mins):** Pizzas are sliced into small portions for a shared family-style buffet so everyone tastes each creation.
3. **Open Dining (45 mins):** Casual seated dinner and drinks.

---

## 3. Cooking Competition (Cook-Off)

### Objective
Friendly competition with team problem-solving and shared tasting/judging.

### Setup & Teams
* **Teams:** 4 teams of 5 (balanced 2–3 men / 2–3 women).
* **Challenge:** 45 minutes to create an appetizer, signature dish, or dessert with provided base ingredients + 1 "mystery ingredient".

### Judging & Categories
* **Judging Panel:** Peer judging (every team rates other dishes on taste, presentation, and creativity).
* **Award Categories:** "Best Flavor", "Most Creative", "Best Team Spirit".
