# Budget & Logistics Tracker

Cost estimates and logistics planning for a 20-person event.

---

## Expense Breakdown (Estimated for 20 Guests)

| Item | Estimated Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Venue / Court Rental** | $100 – $300 | 2–3 courts (Pickleball) or kitchen rental |
| **Food & Ingredients** | $150 – $250 | Pizza ingredients / challenge pantry items |
| **Beverages (Beer/Wine/Non-Alc)** | $100 – $200 | Refreshments & mixers |
| **Supplies & Disposables** | $30 – $50 | Plates, napkins, cups, cutlery |
| **Prizes / Extras** | $20 – $50 | Fun trophies or small prizes |
| **TOTAL** | **$400 – $850** | |

### Cost per Person: ~$20 – $40 / attendee

---

## Venue Requirements Checklist

- [ ] Capacity to host 20 people comfortably
- [ ] Dedicated seating / hangout lounge area
- [ ] Adequate refrigeration and prep space (for food events)
- [ ] Sound system / playlist setup
- [ ] Restroom facilities & parking access
