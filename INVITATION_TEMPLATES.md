# Invitation & Outreach Templates

Casual, low-pressure message templates for inviting the 10 women and 10 men.

---

## Template 1: Direct Personal Invite (SMS / WhatsApp)

> *"Hey [Name]! A few of us are putting together a fun, curated social evening for about 20 single friends (10 guys, 10 girls) to hang out, do [Pickleball / Pizza Making / Cooking], and meet new people in a super chill, zero-pressure setting. No awkward speed dating vibe — just good people, drinks, and fun activities.*
> 
> *Targeting [Date] around [Time] at [Location]. Would love to have you there! Let me know if you're free and I'll count you in."*

---
